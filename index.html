What do the efficiency numbers mean?
Adjusted for charge levelDistance ÷ the energy this trip actually used, worked out from what you added at the charger and the battery % you used. Includes charging losses and stays fair when you don't charge back to the same level.
Tesla's readingDistance ÷ energy from Tesla's own trip screen. Excludes charging losses, so it's usually higher.
